# Executive Presentation: Chicago Taxi Fleet Optimization Strategy

This directory contains the executive presentation deck developed for municipal transit stakeholders and fleet operators.

---

## Deliverable Overview

* **Document:** [`chicago_taxi_optimization_strategy.pdf`](chicago_taxi_optimization_strategy.pdf)
* **Audience:** Municipal transit directors, fleet operations managers, and business stakeholders.
* **Scope:** 
  * Synthesis of 2023 empirical taxi ridership and trip dynamics from Google BigQuery.
  * Spatial-temporal demand patterns and fleet positioning recommendations.
  * Gratuity patterns and electronic payment adoption dynamics.
  * Machine learning integration strategies for real-time fare predictability and driver yield optimization.
